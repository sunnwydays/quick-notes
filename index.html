Log in

Log in with the same email on each device to sync notes between them.

Help

Tap a category to read it, hold one to write. Notes save on this device, and sync to your other devices if you log in.

  • Hold a category until it turns black, then let go, to write (or tap Write first). Save or your phone's back button saves the note.
  • The cloud at the top shows sync: a tick when synced, a line through it when not. Tap it for details.
  • Show times is remembered for each category separately.
  • On the notes screen, double-tap or hold blank space to go back.
  • Search looks through every category. Tap a category heading in the results to open it.
  • Deleting a note or discarding a draft shows an Undo.
  • Schedule: paste a plan, or type a line per block like 9:00-11:30 Foosball. A line starting with a day (Mon) starts that day's schedule, so Sun saved on a Saturday is tomorrow's; without one it's today's. Use ‹ › to see other days. The block on now is in black and finished ones fold away. Edit saves a new version, and every version stays under All saved.
