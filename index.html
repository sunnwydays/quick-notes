Help

What this is

Quick Notes is for getting a thought down fast, usually by voice. Tap a category and the keyboard is already up, ready for typing or the mic. Notes are saved on this device. Log in and they also sync to your other devices.

Write a note

  • Tap a category button. The text box opens with - in it.
  • Enter starts a new - line.
  • Save, or your phone's back button, saves the note and goes home.
  • Discard throws it away. An Undo bar appears on the home screen if you change your mind.
  • Tick Split each line into its own note to save every line as a separate note.

Read your notes

  • Hold a category button for a moment, or tap Read and then the category. Newest notes come first. Tap Read again to cancel.
  • Add writes a new note in the category you are looking at.
  • Show times shows when each note was written. Copy all copies the category's notes.
  • The trash icon deletes a note. Undo brings it back.
  • Remove project (projects only) deletes the project and its notes.

Going back

  • The Back button or your phone's back button.
  • On a category's notes: double-tap anywhere that is not a button, or hold blank space. Holding a note still selects its text.

Search

Type in the search box on the home screen. The category buttons are replaced by every note that contains all the words you typed, grouped by category. Tap a category's heading to open its notes. Clear brings the buttons back.

Projects

+ project adds your own category. Projects sit above the built-in ones.

Back up and sync

  • Export downloads all your notes as a text file.
  • Log in with your email and the code you are sent. Use the same email on each device to sync. The line at the top of the home screen shows the sync state.
  • The app works offline. Changes sync when you are back online.
