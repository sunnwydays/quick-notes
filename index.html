Log in

Log in with the same email on each device to sync notes between them.

Help

Tap a category to read it, hold one to write. Notes save on this device, and sync to your other devices if you log in.

  • Add note writes without picking a category. Start a note with its folder (bring blender, thought …, journal …, a project's name) and it goes there, minus that word. I think … goes to Thought and today was … to Journal, word kept. Anything else goes to Todo.
  • A note that's already in its category isn't added twice.
  • Hold a category until it turns black, then let go, to write (or tap Write first). Enter adds the note and starts the next one. Save or your phone's back button saves what's left and goes back.
  • The cloud at the top shows sync: a tick when synced, a line through it when not. Tap it for details.
  • Show times is remembered for each category separately.
  • On the notes screen, double-tap or hold blank space to go back.
  • Search looks through every category. Tap a category heading in the results to open it.
  • Deleting a note or discarding a draft shows an Undo.
  • Replace (the two arrows next to Delete) deletes a note and opens the text box for a new one, for ticking off a task and writing the next.
  • Schedule: paste a plan, or type a line per block like 9:00-11:30 Foosball. A line starting with a day (Mon) starts that day's schedule, so Sun saved on a Saturday is tomorrow's; without one it's today's. Use ‹ › or swipe sideways to see other days. The block on now is in black and finished ones fold away. New starts a blank one for the next day without a schedule and Edit changes the current one; either saves a new version, and every version stays under All saved.
